# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in the Pifeon community to be welcoming and respectful.
Everyone should be able to contribute without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code constructively; do not attack people.
- Welcome differing perspectives and experiences.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, and publishing
private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the
contact method on the repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not report
conduct concerns in public issues. Maintainers will review reports and take
appropriate action.
